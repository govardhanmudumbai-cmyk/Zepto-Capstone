ZEPT0 MEMBERSHIP POLICY

Zepto offers three account tiers.

Basic is free and is the default tier.

Zepto Pass costs INR 49 per month and provides free standard delivery
on all orders and 5 percent off select categories.

Zepto Pass+ costs INR 99 per month and provides free priority delivery,
10 percent off select categories, and early access to limited-time deals.

Membership can be cancelled at any time from account settings.

Cancelling stops the next billing cycle but does not refund the
current membership period.
