ZEPT0 DELIVERY POLICY

Zepto delivers grocery and household essentials to serviceable pin codes
within 10 to 30 minutes of order confirmation, depending on the customer's
delivery zone and current order volume.

Standard delivery is free on orders over INR 149.

Orders below INR 149 incur a flat INR 25 delivery fee.

Priority delivery is available at checkout for an additional INR 15.

Zepto does not deliver to addresses outside its listed serviceable pin codes.
